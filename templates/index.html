Pipeline

How it works

  1. 1

    Clean data

    11,712 Kaggle X-rays deduplicated to 5,824 unique images, split 69/16/15 by patient.

  2. 2

    Preprocess

    Grayscale, resized to 224×224, scaled to [0, 1], light augmentation in training.

  3. 3

    Train CNNs

    A custom 4-block CNN (8 conv layers) and an ImageNet DenseNet121, fine-tuned in two stages.

  4. 4

    Predict

    Sigmoid output gives P(pneumonia), compared to a 0.5 or Youden threshold.

  5. 5

    Explain

    LIME perturbs superpixels; SHAP distributes the prediction across image regions.
